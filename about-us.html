Company Profile

Vantrex Solutions

Vantrex Solutions is a complete construction supply company providing cement, steel, bricks, sand & aggregate, paint & chemicals, and hardware & tools – along with plumbing, electrical, interior and infrastructure materials.

We serve contractors, builders, developers, architects and distributors who need genuine products, fair prices and on-time delivery. Alongside domestic supply, we also handle import and export trading of construction materials.

The name Vantrex stands for strength, vision and trust – the values behind every order we fulfil.
